Getting a usable result

A smile where the teeth are actually visible and in focus gives the edit something to work on. Ask for "natural white", not "pure white" — real enamel is slightly translucent at the edges.

Braces, gaps and crooked teeth stay exactly as they are. This changes colour, not shape.

A prompt that works

Whiten only this person's teeth — the same teeth, in the same positions and with the same shapes, edges and small natural irregularities, but a clean bright natural white with any yellow or grey staining gone, keeping realistic enamel translucency at the biting edges and soft natural shading between the teeth and near the gums so they do not look like a flat white block. Keep the exact same face, expression, lips, gums, hair, lighting and background completely unchanged; change nothing but the colour of the teeth. Photorealistic, same framing.
